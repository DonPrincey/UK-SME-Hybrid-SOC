# Project Beacon Stakeholder Register

## Purpose

This register identifies the fictional stakeholders involved in the
planning, operation and governance of Project Beacon.

It also explains who may need to participate in security investigations,
containment decisions, communications and incident recovery.

## Stakeholder register

| Stakeholder | Primary responsibility | Interest in the project | Influence | Communication needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Executive Sponsor | Approves the programme, budget and major business decisions | Reduced organisational risk and clear business value | High | Executive summaries, major risks and progress updates |
| Project Lead / SOC Analyst | Designs the SOC, investigates alerts and maintains project documentation | Successful technical delivery and usable security processes | High | Technical records, task tracking and decision logs |
| IT Manager | Oversees IT operations and approves technical changes | Reliable systems, manageable controls and reduced disruption | High | Architecture decisions, incidents and change requests |
| Systems Administrator | Manages servers, accounts, endpoints and technical configuration | Clear technical requirements and safe implementation | High | Technical instructions, alerts and containment actions |
| Service Desk | Receives user reports and supports initial incident triage | Clear escalation procedures and practical runbooks | Medium | Alert guidance, escalation criteria and user communications |
| Data Protection and Compliance Representative | Assesses privacy, legal and contractual impact | Protection of customer and employee information | High | Data-impact assessments, incident updates and evidence |
| Human Resources | Supports employee-related investigations and communications | Fair handling of employee matters and business continuity | Medium | Approved communications and investigation outcomes |
| Department Managers | Manage affected teams and business operations | Minimum disruption and clear recovery expectations | Medium | Incident impact, recovery timelines and staff instructions |
| Employees | Use company systems and report suspicious activity | Safe access to systems and clear security guidance | Low | Awareness messages, reporting instructions and updates |
| External Technology Provider | Supports cloud, software or infrastructure services | Clear responsibilities and timely technical coordination | Medium | Technical evidence, support requests and escalation details |
| Customers | Depend on the confidentiality and availability of services | Protection of information and continued service | Medium | Approved external communications where required |
| Executive Leadership Team | Makes strategic and high-impact response decisions | Business continuity, reputation and financial protection | High | Business impact, major decisions and recovery status |

## Stakeholder groups

### Decision-makers

- Executive Sponsor
- Executive Leadership Team
- IT Manager
- Data Protection and Compliance Representative

### Technical responders

- Project Lead / SOC Analyst
- Systems Administrator
- Service Desk
- External Technology Provider

### Business participants

- Human Resources
- Department Managers
- Employees

### Potentially affected external parties

- Customers
- Technology suppliers
- Contractual partners

## Incident responsibilities

During a security incident:

- The SOC Analyst investigates alerts and gathers evidence.
- The Systems Administrator supports technical containment.
- The IT Manager approves significant operational changes.
- The Executive Sponsor approves high-impact business decisions.
- The Compliance Representative assesses privacy and reporting obligations.
- Human Resources supports incidents involving employees.
- Department Managers coordinate affected business teams.
- The Service Desk receives user reports and distributes approved instructions.
- External providers support systems under their responsibility.

## Communication principles

Project Beacon will use the following communication principles:

- Technical details will be provided to technical responders.
- Business impact will be explained to senior management.
- Personal and sensitive information will be shared only when necessary.
- External communications will require appropriate approval.
- Incident updates will clearly state what is known, unknown and being investigated.
- Communication records will be maintained for significant incidents.

## Stakeholder engagement approach

| Stakeholder group | Engagement method | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Executive leadership | Executive briefing | At milestones and during major incidents |
| IT and security personnel | Technical review | Weekly or during active incidents |
| Compliance and HR | Risk and impact consultation | When incidents involve data or employees |
| Employees | Security awareness and instructions | As required |
| External providers | Support tickets and technical calls | When external assistance is required |
| Customers | Approved formal communication | Only when necessary |
